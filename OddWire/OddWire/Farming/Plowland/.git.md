**Scope:** Feature

---

## What it is
A plow tool plus a soil-nutrition block entity that converts soil into high-retention furrowed farmland: it tracks the support block below to drive moisture retention, hosts a crop, and drains NPK across neighbouring soil as the crop grows.

---

## Core Loop

```
Hold-plow soil → place plowland furrow (fertility roll) → plant → grow on retained moisture + NPK → re-plow to reset
```

Inputs: **plow** (tool — durability per plow), **target soil/farmland** (fertility + nutrient source), **support block below** (sets moisture retention)
Output: **plowland furrow** — its NPK is `min(target nutrients + support max, target-tier value)`, so support **refills drained nutrients on re-plow** rather than boosting fresh soil above its tier; it also reverts its two perpendicular neighbours to plain farmland. The plow loses durability each plow; the target's surface weeds/grass are consumed into a slow-release bonus.

Drag-plowing fires once the hold passes `PlowSecondsRequired` (0.6s) and the target moves off the last plowed block. Moisture and crop growth advance on the vanilla fast-forward interval.

---

## Driving Values

| Value | What drives it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Nutrients (N/P/K) | inherited from target + support on plow; drained by crop growth; recovered toward original by vanilla soil logic | feed vanilla `GetGrowthRate`; their average decides the plow fertility roll |
| Moisture | watering / rain vs drying over `totalHoursWaterRetention` | gates growth (stalls below 0.1) and sets the dry/moist block variant |
| Support retention | fertility of the soil block directly below | scales `totalHoursWaterRetention` — richer support holds water longer |
| Fertility tier | plow rolls a step up/down from the nutrient average vs the neutral pivot | selects the `plowland-…-{fertility}` variant and the nutrient ceiling |
| Slow-release bonus | weeds, grass cover, and a partially-grown crop consumed on plow | tops up nutrients over time after plowing |

---

## Fertility roll — plow step
On each plow, `chanceChange = (N + P + K + supportMax) / 4`, rolled against `rand·100`:

```
chanceChange < FertilityNeutral(100)  → chance the richer of target/support loses a tier
chanceChange ≥ FertilityNeutral(100)  → chance the poorer of target/support gains a tier
```

Ties and "no support" resolve toward the target block. Low-NPK support may also lose a tier when reverted to soil.

## Gameplay Loop

| Input | Action | Reward | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plow on soil/farmland/plowland | hold right-click, drag across blocks | furrowed plowland with combined target+support nutrients and a slow-release bonus | plow durability spent; underfed soil can drop a fertility tier; perpendicular neighbours revert to farmland |
| Support block below | place richer soil under the furrow | longer moisture retention, more harvests per watering | poor/over-dry support dries the furrow out faster |
| Crop on the furrow | plant, water, wait | faster crop cycling from sustained NPK + moisture | growth stalls below 0.1 moisture; growth halts in darkness; sustained cold/heat damages drops and ultimately kills the crop (full Farmland parity) |

## Class Responsibility

| Class | Owns |
|---|---|
| `BlockEntityPlowland` | support tracking, moisture-retention wiring, crop hosting (via `CropGrowth`), distributed NPK drain, block-variant updates, persistence |
| `BlockPlowland` | solid-face / crop-attachment rules, interact routing to the block entity |
| `ItemPlow` | plow tool — target resolution, fertility roll, nutrient transfer, furrow placement, neighbour revert |
| `PlowlandSettings` | tuning constants only (retention, caps, fertility pivot, plow timing) |
| `CropGrowth` | shared, portable crop layer — growth, light, damage/death, drops, persistence; mirrors `BlockEntityFarmland` so it can host crops without native Farmland (`_Environment/`) |
| `FertilitySet` | shared fertility-code/value/step helpers and support-revert logic (`_Environment/`) |
