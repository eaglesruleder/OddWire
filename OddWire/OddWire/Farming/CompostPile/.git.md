# Brief — CompostPile

**Scope:** Feature
**Domain:** OddWire.Farming
**Status:** Active

---

## What it is
A tick-driven organic-decomposition simulation: a two-stage pipeline turns browns, nutrition, and inoculum into compost, with four environmental axes — temperature, moisture, aeration, and an inoculum culture — setting the rate.

---

## Core Loop

```
Place pile → Add browns / inoculum / whole piles, feed nutrition → Maintain moisture → Wait (decompose, then compost) → Break to harvest in layers
```

Inputs: **browns** (drygrass — bulk carbon, master feedstock), **inoculum** (rot/compost — culture seed), **nutrition** (perishable foods — accelerant)
Output: **compost**, plus recoverable browns / inoculum / whole-pile blocks on break. Nutrition is lossy — never recovered.

Server-ticks every 12s; progression is elapsed-time based and catches up after a chunk reloads.

---

## Driving Values

| Value | What drives it | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Temperature | environment + biological self-heat + adjacent heat sources | gates both stages; cold or hot slows it, overheating stresses it |
| Moisture | rain / watering vs ambient drying, over a retention floor | drives compost rate; dry or drowning slows and stresses it |
| Aeration | decays with fullness, blocked neighbours, and oxygen demand; restored by adding material | drives decomposition; low aeration calls for a turn |
| Inoculum culture | produced by decomposition, burned by composting | gates and scales the compost rate |
| Nutrition mix | fed foods, by category | accelerates inoculum yield and self-heat; raises oxygen demand |

---

## Central gate — Seeded culture
Composting produces nothing until the culture is seeded: while inoculum is below one transition's worth, the compost rate is zero (shown as **Unseeded**).

---

## Output resolution — Inoculum equilibrium
Decomposition makes inoculum by consuming nutrition (then browns); composting burns inoculum at a rate that rises with the inoculum level. The two settle at a self-limiting equilibrium below ~50%. Feeding nutrition lifts the equilibrium and overall throughput — it never runs away.

Conversions: nutrition/browns → inoculum (capped by available material and pool room); 12 browns + 2 inoculum → 1 compost (capped by the scarcest of browns, inoculum, output room). Browns feed both stages, so they deplete fastest.

---

## Gameplay Loop

| Input | Action | Reward | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| Browns / inoculum / pile blocks | right-click to add (Ctrl = ×5) | feeds the pipeline, restores aeration | surplus over capacity drops back as items |
| Food | right-click to feed | faster culture + compost | consumed, never returned |
| Water / rain | keep moisture in band | sustains the rate | over-wet + airless = drowning |
| Break the block | dig it up (shovel hinted) | layered drops: compost, pile blocks, browns, inoculum | nutrition inside is lost |
