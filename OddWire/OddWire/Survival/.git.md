## What it is
The survival quality-of-life domain: vanilla-system patches that adjust inventory and interaction behaviour. Currently one feature — small bags carried in the hotbar.

---

## Features

| Feature | What it is | Brief | Doc |
|---|---|---|---|
| Handheld Bag | lets small bags (< 4 slots, e.g. a basket) be held in the hotbar and filled by right-click block pickup and inventory merge — trading a tool slot for a little carry capacity | [.git.md](_Patches/BBRClickPickup/.git.md) | [.gpt.md](_Patches/BBRClickPickup/.gpt.md) |

---

## Class Responsibility

| Class | Owns |
|---|---|
| `IHeldBag_Extensions` | `IsHandheld` — small-bag discriminator (< 4 slots) |
| `BBRightClickPickup_BasketIntercept_Patch` | routes right-click block drops into a held bag first |
| `CollectibleObject_..._Patch` (×2) | makes a held bag a valid direct-merge target and performs the item→bag merge |
| `ItemSlot_..._Patch` / `ItemSlotSurvival_..._Patch` (×3) | bag-in-slot swap, and allowing filled small bags in survival/hotbar slots |
